Earth's Warming Fever, 1850–2014

A temperature anomaly measures how much warmer or cooler a given year was compared to the pre-industrial average (1850–1900), the reference period before large-scale industrialization began altering the climate.

A value of zero means that year matched the baseline. Positive values indicate warming above it. The faint line shows raw year-by-year data, and the bold purple line defaults to a 30-year rolling average, the standard used by climate scientists (IPCC) to filter out short-term variability and reveal the underlying long-term trend. The stripes below encode each year as a color: blue for cooler, red for warmer.

Drag across any part of the chart to select a time range. The distribution and statistics below will update to reflect only the years you selected, so you can compare different eras side by side.
