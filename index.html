Earth's Warming Fever, 1850–2014

A temperature anomaly is just how much warmer or cooler a given year was compared to the pre-industrial average (1850-1900, before fossil fuels really took off).

Zero means that year matched the old normal. Positive means warmer than that baseline. The faint line shows raw year-by-year data, and the bold purple line defaults to a 30-year rolling average, which is the standard climate scientists (IPCC) use to smooth out short-term noise and see the actual long-term trend. The stripes below show each year as a color: blue for cooler, red for warmer.

Drag across any part of the chart to select a time range. The distribution and statistics below will update to reflect only the years you selected, so you can compare different eras side by side.
